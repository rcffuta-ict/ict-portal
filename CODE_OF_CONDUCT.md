# Code of conduct

Everyone who works on the portal serves the fellowship through it. We work together the
way the fellowship asks us to live: with love, honesty and humility (Colossians 3:12–14).

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new or still learning.
- Criticise work, never people. Explain why, and offer a better way.
- Own your mistakes and say so early. A broken deploy you mention is easy to fix; one
  you hide is not.
- Hand things over properly. Leave notes, access and documentation so the next person
  isn't left guessing.

## Member data is a trust

The portal holds the personal details of our brothers and sisters.

- Look at member data only when your work needs it.
- Never copy member data to personal devices, spreadsheets or chats beyond what the
  task requires, and delete those copies afterwards.
- Never use the portal or its data for anything outside the fellowship's work.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, mockery, discrimination, sharing someone's private information, or using
access you were given for anything it wasn't given for.

## Raising a concern

Speak to the ICT Coordinator. If the concern is about the ICT Coordinator, speak to the
VP Admin or the President. Concerns are handled privately and fairly.
